# Crunchy Trail – art direction
- Soft pastel 3D "clay / mochi" render, rounded shapes, studio lighting, subtle ambient occlusion, gentle depth of field.
- Palette: cream #FFF6EC, peach #FFB9A3, mint #9EE6CF, lilac #C9B6FF, sky #A8D8FF, butter #FFE59A, coral accent #FF7A8A.
- Hero: "Mochi" – a round, squishy, cream-white blob creature (slightly flattened sphere), two small glossy black bead eyes with white highlights, rosy pink blush cheeks, tiny happy mouth, two stubby little feet. No arms. Cute, kawaii, toy-like.
- World: floating pastel islands covered with satisfying materials (bubble wrap, lawn grass, snow, autumn leaves, kinetic sand, jelly slime cubes, ice crystals, kalimba keys).
